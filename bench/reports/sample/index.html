How to read this report

enc/dec fps (st)
Single-thread frames per second, median of 5 pinned-core runs.
enc/dec fps (mt)
Multi-thread wall-clock frames per second across min(8, hw_concurrency) workers.
ratio
Total payload bytes divided by total raw bytes; smaller is better. 0.30 means a 3.3:1 compression.
PSNR / SSIM
Quality vs ground truth. For lossless profiles PSNR is ∞ (RMSE = 0).
PC diff %
Fraction of pixels whose reprojected 3D position moved more than 5 mm under the codec's reconstruction.
